Careers & Postsecondary Pathways

Choose courses with prerequisites and future options in mind. Good planning keeps several destinations open.

🎓 University

Programs often require specific Grade 12 U or M courses.

  • Check prerequisites early
  • Build strong writing and quantitative skills
  • Review admission averages and supplementary applications

🏫 College

Programs may require C, M, or U courses depending on field.

  • Compare diplomas, degrees, and certificates
  • Look for labs, placements, and transfer options
  • Confirm mathematics and science prerequisites

🛠️ Apprenticeship

Combine paid workplace training with classroom learning in a skilled trade.

  • Explore co-op and OYAP
  • Develop safety and technical skills
  • Understand certification pathways

💼 Workplace

Prepare for employment, training, and future upskilling.

  • Résumé and interview skills
  • Communication and teamwork
  • Digital, financial, and workplace literacy

Career-readiness checklist

Missing but essential skills

Workplace communication

Email, meetings, feedback, documentation, respectful disagreement, and asking for help.

Professional habits

Punctuality, reliability, safety, integrity, organization, and completing commitments.

Decision-making

Compare cost, time, fit, opportunity, risk, and long-term flexibility—not prestige alone.
