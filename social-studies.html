Canadian history anchor timeline

A starting framework—not a substitute for detailed study or Indigenous perspectives.

Before 1500

Diverse Indigenous nations and civilizations developed political, economic, cultural, and ecological systems across the land now called Canada.

1500s–1800s

Contact, trade, alliance, conflict, settlement, colonial rule, displacement, treaties, and resistance reshaped North America.

1867

Canadian Confederation created the Dominion of Canada; expansion and federal policies had profound consequences for Indigenous peoples and other communities.

1914–1945

Two world wars, the Great Depression, rights struggles, and changing international roles transformed Canada.

1945–present

Immigration, social movements, constitutional debate, globalization, reconciliation, and environmental challenges shaped contemporary Canada.
