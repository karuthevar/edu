⚡ Electricity Basics

Watch electric charge move and see how current, voltage, resistance, magnetism, induction, and Ohm's Law connect.

Current

1. Electric Current

Current is the movement of electric charge through a circuit.

Voltage

2. Voltage

Voltage is the electrical push that helps charges move through a circuit.

Resistance

3. Resistance

Resistance makes it harder for electric charge to move.

Magnetism

4. Current Creates Magnetism

Electric current in a wire creates a magnetic field around the wire.

Induction

5. Electromagnetic Induction

A changing magnetic field near a wire or coil can produce an electric current.

Ohm's Law

6. Ohm's Law

Voltage, current, and resistance are connected: changing one can affect the others.
