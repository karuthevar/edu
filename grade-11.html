Grade 11 Science Pathways

Students typically specialize. This page shows the major university-preparation science pathways.

Grade 11

Chemistry SCH3U

Matter, chemical trends, bonding, chemical reactions, quantities in chemical reactions, solutions, and gases.

Grade 11

Physics SPH3U

Kinematics, forces, Newton's three laws, energy and society, waves and sound, and electricity/magnetism.

Grade 11

Biology SBI3U

Diversity of living things, evolution, genetic processes, animals, and plants.

Grade 11

Newton's Laws in Depth

Free-body diagrams, net force, F = ma, friction, weight, and action-reaction force pairs.

Grade 11

Chemistry Quantities

Moles, molar mass, stoichiometric relationships, concentration, and gas relationships.
