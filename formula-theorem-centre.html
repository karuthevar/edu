Seven-step formula method

  1. List knowns and unknowns.
  2. Choose a relationship matching the conditions.
  3. Rearrange.
  4. Convert units.
  5. Substitute with brackets.
  6. Calculate and round at the end.
  7. State units and check reasonableness.
