Forum Community Rules

The forum is for school learning, course planning, study strategies, and respectful student support.

1. Protect privacy

Use a nickname. Do not post full names, phone numbers, home or school addresses, schedules, private account handles, passwords, or live location.

2. Be respectful

No bullying, harassment, hate, threats, humiliation, or targeting people based on identity or ability.

3. Keep it school-safe

No sexual content, graphic violence, dangerous challenges, illegal activity, drug promotion, gambling, or instructions that could cause harm.

4. Support learning

Explain ideas and steps. Do not post leaked tests, copied answer keys, or claim another person's work as your own.

5. No private solicitation

No requests to meet, move to private chats, exchange contact details, advertise tutoring without approval, or conduct transactions.

6. Report concerns

Use the report button for unsafe content. Moderators may hide posts, lock discussions, suspend accounts, and preserve records needed for safety review.

Emergency or immediate safety concern: Tell a trusted adult or appropriate local authority. The forum is not an emergency service.
